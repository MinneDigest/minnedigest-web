Key Pricing Considerations

1. Value-Based Pricing: MinneStudio saves journalists 10-15 hours/week on content production. At an average journalist salary of $60k/year (~$30/hour), that's $450-600/week in labor savings.

2. Competitive Analysis:

  • NotebookLM: Free (but limited, no custom features)
  • GenSpark: Free (but no API, no newsroom features)
  • Professional podcast production: $200-500 per episode
  • Newsletter automation tools: $50-200/month

3. Profitability Targets:

  • Minimum 200% profit margin on all tiers
  • Break-even: 2-3 customers per tier
  • Target: 20-50 newsroom clients in Year 1
  • Projected Annual Revenue: $150k-$450k

4. Scaling Economics:

  • Volume discounts with ElevenLabs at 10+ clients
  • Bulk API pricing with OpenAI/Google at scale
  • Infrastructure costs grow sub-linearly with customers
  • Estimated margin improvement: +5-10% at 20+ clients

💰 Bottom Line Recommendation

Start with $299/month for small newsrooms, scaling to $1,499/month for enterprises. This provides healthy margins (200-300%) while remaining competitive and delivering exceptional value.
